How it works

Connect once. Sell forever.

No code, no new app for your customers, no ops burden for you.

STEP 01

Connect your Shopify store

One-time OAuth connect. Your entire catalog, prices, images and live inventory sync automatically — and stay synced via webhooks.

STEP 02

Share your WhatsApp number

Customers say "hi" and get a guided storefront: categories, product cards with photos, and a cart — powered by the official WhatsApp Cloud API.

STEP 03

Get paid, auto-fulfill

UPI payment links via Razorpay, instant order confirmation in chat, and the order lands in Shopify for your normal fulfilment flow.

Features

Everything between "hi" and "delivered".

🛒

In-chat checkout

Browse → cart → address → UPI payment, entirely inside WhatsApp. No redirects, no login walls, no drop-off.

🔄

Cart recovery on autopilot

Abandoned carts get a smart nudge with a one-tap resume button. Recovered revenue shows up on your dashboard.

🖼️

Rich product cards

Real product photos from your Shopify CDN, prices, discounts and stock — rendered natively in the chat.

📊

Merchant dashboard

GMV, orders, conversion, AOV, recovery rate and a live order feed — per store, in real time.

📦

Order tracking in chat

"track" shows customers their recent orders and status. Shipping updates arrive in the same thread they bought in.

Multi-tenant by design
